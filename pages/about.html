OUR STORY

Bringing Nature
Closer to You

GreenLeaf started as a simple idea between a group of plant lovers who wanted fresh, healthy plants to be just a click away. Today we grow, pack and deliver indoor, outdoor, flowering and medicinal plants to homes across the country.

Every plant that leaves our nursery is checked for quality and packed with care, so it reaches you happy and ready to grow. Our goal is to make buying plants easy, affordable and enjoyable for everyone.
